HKB MODEL
HAKEN · KELSO · BUNZ 1985

How it works

Move your finger up and down on the left track. The HKB oscillator on the right follows according to the coordination dynamics equation:

dΦ/dt = −a·sin(Φ) − 2b·sin(2Φ) + noise

a controls in-phase stability. b controls anti-phase stability. When b/a is small, only in-phase (Φ≈0) is stable — the classic phase transition.

Use Auto mode and increase frequency to watch the spontaneous transition from anti-phase to in-phase coordination.
